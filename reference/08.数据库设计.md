### 2. 决策与交易闭环 (Decision & Trade Loop)

复盘系统的数据源头，严格关联 AI 建议与用户的真实执行。

| 表名 (Table) | 核心字段设计 (字段名 / 类型 / 描述) |
| --- | --- |
| `ai_suggestions` | `id`, `user_id`, `symbol`, `suggested_action` (Buy/Sell), `target_price` (Numeric), `suggested_qty` (Int), `logic_summary` (Text), `status` (Enum: 待执行/已执行/已忽略) |
| `trade_logs` | `id`, `user_id`, `suggestion_id` (FK, **关键关联**), `actual_action` (Varchar), `executed_price` (Numeric), `executed_qty` (Int), `executed_at` (Timestamp) |
| `post_mortem` | `id`, `suggestion_id` (FK), `trade_log_id` (FK), `error_type` (Enum: AI策略失效/用户未执行), `lesson_summary` (Text) |

### 3. 引擎舱与认知记忆 (Engine & Memory)

| 表名 (Table) | 核心字段设计 (字段名 / 类型 / 描述) |

| `vector_memory` | `id`, `user_id`, `content` (Text), `embedding` (**vector(1536)**), `tags` (Array) |
